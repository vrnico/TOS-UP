# LinkedIn User Agreement

**Effective: November 3, 2025**

## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Contract Formation
By accessing LinkedIn's services, you enter a legally binding contract. This applies whether using personal credentials or company-provided access. Non-agreement means you shouldn't create an account.

The contract covers LinkedIn.com, LinkedIn apps, and related services collecting data through plugins like "Apply with LinkedIn."

**Entity Details:**
- EU/EEA/Switzerland residents: Contract with LinkedIn Ireland Unlimited Company
- Others: Contract with LinkedIn Corporation
- Affiliates include Microsoft subsidiaries

**Key Definitions:**
Social Action covers likes, comments, follows, and content sharing. Content encompasses feed posts, profiles, articles, videos, messages, and PDFs.

### 1.2 Members and Visitors
Members register for full access; Visitors access limited features without registration.

### 1.3 Changes to Terms
LinkedIn may modify terms with notice, except when legal requirements demand immediate implementation or new features launch. Changes aren't retroactive. Continued use indicates acceptance.

## 2. Obligations

### 2.1 Service Eligibility
Users must be at least 16 years old (or older if local law requires). You agree to maintain one account in your real name and avoid false identity creation.

### 2.2 Your Account
You must protect passwords, prevent unauthorized access, and not share accounts. You're responsible for account activity unless reported as misused. Employers purchasing services for work may control access but cannot claim personal account rights.

### 2.3 Payment
Users agree to pay applicable fees and taxes. LinkedIn may store payment methods and auto-renew subscriptions. Foreign exchange fees may apply. Refunds follow LinkedIn's policy.

### 2.4 Notices and Messages
LinkedIn communicates through the platform or registered contact information. You're responsible for keeping contact details current.

### 2.5 Sharing
Shared information becomes visible to others based on your privacy settings. LinkedIn respects audience choices (e.g., "connections only"). Job applications default to private visibility unless you signal interest publicly.

## 3. Rights and Limits

### 3.1 Your License to LinkedIn
You own original content submitted, granting LinkedIn a non-exclusive, worldwide license to use, copy, modify, and display it without additional compensation.

**License Limits:**
- Delete content anytime, except copies already shared or sublicensed remain with others
- LinkedIn won't include your content in third-party ads without consent
- Ads appear near your content; social actions may accompany ads per the Privacy Policy
- Content shared publicly may be redistributed off-platform or made searchable
- LinkedIn edits content for format (translation, transcription, resizing) while preserving meaning
- You can license content under Creative Commons or similar frameworks

You promise content is truthful and doesn't violate laws or rights. LinkedIn may remove content legally required in certain jurisdictions.

### 3.2 Service Availability
LinkedIn may change, suspend, or discontinue features and services by country or subscription level. LinkedIn isn't obligated to store or display content; it's "not a storage service."

### 3.3 Other Content, Sites, and Apps
LinkedIn isn't responsible for inaccurate, incomplete, or harmful third-party content. Members offering services, employment, or products aren't LinkedIn employees or agents. LinkedIn doesn't supervise these offers, employment decisions, or service delivery.

**Services Marketplace:** Users must be 18+ and possess required licenses.

**Events:** LinkedIn doesn't vet events, speakers, or attendee conduct.

### 3.4 Limits on Use
LinkedIn reserves rights to limit connections, messaging, and feature access. Accounts may be restricted, suspended, or terminated for contract violations, legal violations, or service misuse.

### 3.5 Intellectual Property Rights
LinkedIn retains all intellectual property in the services. LinkedIn trademarks are registered marks of LinkedIn.

### 3.6 Recommendations and Automated Processing
LinkedIn uses data to recommend connections, content, ads, and features. Keeping profiles accurate improves recommendations.

**Generative AI Features:** Auto-generated content may be inaccurate or incomplete. Users must review before sharing and ensure compliance with community policies.

Users verify content accuracy and fitness for their purposes.

### 4.2 Exclusion of Liability
LinkedIn isn't liable for lost profits, reputation harm, data loss, or indirect damages. Liability caps at either total fees paid or $1,000 USD, whichever is greater.

### 4.3 Basis of the Bargain
Limitations apply to all liability claims. **Exceptions:** Death, personal injury, fraud, gross negligence, intentional misconduct, or material obligation breaches remain actionable.

## 5. Termination

Both parties may terminate anytime. Upon termination:
- Feedback rights remain with LinkedIn
- Section 3 survives (with exceptions)
- Sections 4, 6, 7, and 8.2 survive
- Outstanding payment obligations persist

Users can close accounts via the Help Center.

## 6. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

**Designated Countries (EU/EEA/Switzerland):**
Irish law governs all claims, but mandatory consumer protections apply. Disputes go to courts where services are directed to your habitual residence or Irish courts.

DMA business users may use alternative dispute resolution in the Help Center.

**Outside Designated Countries:**
California law governs exclusively. All disputes litigate in Santa Clara County, California federal/state courts only.

Users may have additional appeal rights for certain LinkedIn decisions.
