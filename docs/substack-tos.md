# Substack Terms of Use

**Effective: April 21, 2025**

## Overview

Substack Inc. presents binding contractual terms governing platform usage. The agreement encompasses the main Terms of Use plus linked documents including Privacy Policy, Publisher Agreement, Content Guidelines, Support Chatbot Terms, and Copyright Dispute Policy.

## Account Requirements
Users must be at least 16 years old. Account holders provide accurate registration information and agree not to transfer accounts without written permission. Impersonation and name misappropriation are prohibited.

## Content Ownership & Licensing
Creators retain ownership of original posts. Users grant Substack a "royalty-free, perpetual, irrevocable, and worldwide" license to display and modify content for technical adaptation across devices.

## Prohibited Activities
The platform restricts: infringement, account security jeopardy, unauthorized access attempts, network disruption, automated scraping, data copying, and reverse engineering efforts.

## Liability Limitations
The platform disclaims liability for indirect damages and caps monetary responsibility at the greater of $100 or amounts paid within twelve months preceding claims.

## Dispute Resolution
Disputes proceed through arbitration in San Francisco County under JAMS rules. Class action participation is waived. California law governs the agreement.

## Account Termination
Substack may terminate accounts at discretion. Users may self-delete, though public posts may remain accessible elsewhere.

## Children's Protection
Per COPPA requirements, the platform doesn't knowingly collect personal information from users under 16.
