# Substack Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: December 1, 2025**

## About this Privacy Policy

Substack Inc. recognizes your concerns regarding personal information use and sharing. This policy outlines how personal identifiable information gets collected, used, and shared through www.substack.com and related services. The policy incorporates the Terms of Use, which define undefined terms referenced here.

This Privacy Policy includes additional notices applicable to California consumers (see "Additional Notices for California Residents" section below).

## Policy Scope

This policy details collection, receipt, use, storage, sharing, transfer, and processing of personal information. It describes your choices, rights, and how to exercise them.

The policy applies only to Substack's processing as a data controller for its purposes. It does not cover Substack's processing as a data processor on behalf of Creators, who maintain separate privacy practices.

## Policy Modifications

Substack may modify this policy as services improve. Changes are communicated through site notices, emails, or other means. Legal notice emails remain binding even if you've opted out of receiving them.

---

## What Information Does Substack Collect?

Substack collects personal information through user interactions and service subscriptions:

- First and last name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Date of birth
- Payment details (billing address, credit card information)
- Location and mailing address
- Photographs
- Marketing preferences and consents
- Browser/device information (IP address)
- Username and password
- Subscription status
- Social media account information
- Direct message contents and metadata
- Integrated third-party service information (YouTube account identifiers, video identifiers, upload status)
- Communication contents when contacting Substack

**Address Book Syncing:** Information collected through address book syncing is limited to hashed email addresses and phone numbers.

**Cookie Data:** Website usage information is collected through cookies.

## How Does Substack Use Personal Information?

Substack processes personal information for these purposes:

**Contract Fulfillment:**
- Establishing and fulfilling contracts with users
- Verifying identity and processing payments
- Providing customer service and communications

**Legitimate Business Interests:**
- Providing requested services and responding to communications
- Monitoring service usage and improving/protecting services
- Creating, maintaining, customizing, and securing user accounts
- Personalizing services and providing recommendations
- Monitoring accounts to prevent fraud, misrepresentation, terrorism, security incidents, and crime
- Inviting users to surveys and market research
- Facilitating contact syncing for opted-in users

**Legal Compliance:**
- Meeting applicable legal requirements including age verification
- Protecting legitimate business interests and legal rights
- Addressing legal claims, compliance, regulatory, and investigative purposes

**Direct Marketing:**
- Sending marketing communications based on user consent
- Users may opt out at any time

## How Does Substack Share Personal Information?

Substack shares personal information with:

**Affiliates:** Subsidiaries and corporate affiliates for described purposes.

**Creators:** When users subscribe to Creator publications, Substack provides necessary information (name and email) enabling content delivery. Creators control their publications; personal information is provided directly to Creators when users interact with publications requiring such information.

**Service Providers:** Third-party providers delivering services on Substack's behalf, including website hosting and maintenance, email services, security services, generative AI services, content delivery networks, customer support operations, traffic and analytics services, cloud storage and computing services.

**Third-Party Data Controllers:** Optional integrations with third-party services. Personal information is used only for providing integrations and isn't disclosed except when legally required or directed by the third party.

**Other Users:** User profiles may display information facilitating user interaction, including names, subscriptions, publications, locations, and images. Public profile data may be accessible through automated means (APIs, indexing services). Account privacy settings limit visibility.

**Prospective Buyers/Sellers:** Personal information may be shared during business sales, mergers, or asset transfers.

**Government Authorities:** Personal information is disclosed to regulatory, prosecuting, and governmental authorities, courts, and litigants when legally mandated or necessary for protecting Substack's legitimate interests.

**Anonymized Information:** Substack may anonymize information preventing individual identification and share such data with trusted partners.

## Privacy and Direct Messages

Substack users can send and receive direct messages. Direct messages are not end-to-end encrypted and are not substitutes for secure messaging services.

Message contents are disclosed to intended recipients. Recipients may retain messages even if deletion is requested or the account is deleted. Substack discloses message contents to non-recipients only in limited circumstances allowed or required by law, such as valid court orders or emergencies involving death or serious physical injury risk.

Substack maintains strict internal access controls on direct messaging. However, Substack personnel may access message contents to enforce Terms of Use, ensure platform security, provide user support, or provide necessary services. Automated means scan messaging content for spam, malicious content, and child abuse material.

## What Are Your Rights?

Depending on applicable local laws, users may request copies of personal information, correct it, erase or restrict its processing, or request transfer to other organizations. Users may object to some processing or request processing restrictions. Users may withdraw consent for processing.

Through account settings, users may access, edit, or delete:
- Name and password
- Email address
- User profile information and uploaded images

To delete accounts, users visit their account page. Account deletion removes associated Posts, though public Posts may remain available.

## How Long Does Substack Retain Data?

Substack retains information only as long as reasonably necessary for fulfillment purposes. Personal information may be retained longer during complaints or when litigation prospects exist.

## Automated Individual Decision-Making, Including Profiling

Substack uses collected information to profile users for suggesting relevant platform content. Automated systems flag content potentially violating Content Guidelines or Terms of Use. Currently, no final content moderation decisions are made without human review of automated decisions.

## Cookies

Substack uses cookies on its website. Categories include:

**Strictly Necessary Cookies:** Essential cookies enabling website navigation and feature use (Cloudflare, AWS, session identifiers).

**Performance Cookies:** Collect website usage information anonymously (Google Analytics, Facebook, Datadog, FullStory).

**Functionality Cookies:** Remember user choices like country, language, and search parameters.

**Creator Cookies:** Creators may set tracking and analytics cookies subject to their terms and policies.

Disabling cookies may prevent completing certain activities or accessing website parts correctly.

Substack services do not support Do Not Track requests currently.
