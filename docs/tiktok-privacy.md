# TikTok Privacy Policy - U.S.

**Last updated: February 5, 2026**

## Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to TikTok services including apps, websites, software, and related services accessed via any platform or device, as well as TikTok Ad Network. Services are operated by TikTok USDS Joint Venture LLC.

The policy explains how TikTok collects, uses, shares, and processes personal information of users and individuals in connection with the Services. For consumer health data under Washington's My Health My Data Act, refer to the separate Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy.

---

## Information We Collect

TikTok collects information through three methods: information you provide, automatically collected information, and information from other sources.

### Information You Provide

Users may provide:

- **Account information**: date of birth, username, password, email, phone number, profile image
- **User content**: comments, photographs, livestreams, audio recordings, videos, text created or uploaded
- **Messages**: content composed, sent, or received through messaging and chat functionalities
- **AI interactions**: prompts, questions, files submitted to AI-powered interfaces
- **Clipboard content**: text and images accessed when using certain sharing functions
- **Purchase information**: payment card data, billing, delivery, contact information, purchase history
- **Contacts**: phone and social network contacts with permission
- **Account settings and communication preferences**
- **Identity verification information**
- **Correspondence with TikTok**
- **Survey and promotional participation information**

**Sensitive Personal Information**: Users may voluntarily include sensitive information in content (racial/ethnic origin, religious beliefs, health information, sexual orientation, citizenship status, financial information). TikTok does not require this information but cautions that published content may be publicly visible.

### Automatically Collected Information

TikTok automatically collects:

- **Usage information**: interaction with content and ads, duration and frequency of service use, engagement with other users, in-app browser activity
- **Technical information**: IP address, user agent, mobile carrier, time zone, advertising identifiers, device model, system information, network type, screen resolution, operating system, app names, keystroke patterns, battery state, audio settings
- **Location information**: approximate location based on device/network information, IP address, SIM card region, device settings; precise location if location services enabled
- **Content characteristics**: identification of objects, scenery, faces, body parts in videos; text of spoken words; biometric identifiers like faceprints and voiceprints
- **Metadata**: creation details, timestamps, usernames enabling content tracing
- **Cookies and similar technologies**: web beacons, flash cookies, pixels, SDKs for functionality measurement, experience enhancement, service improvement, advertising, and promotional purposes

### Information From Other Sources

TikTok may receive information from:

- Third-party sign-up/login services
- Advertisers, publishers, and measurement partners providing external activity data, mobile identifiers, hashed contact information
- TikTok Advertiser Tools integrations (TikTok Pixel)
- Sellers and payment/transaction providers
- Affiliated companies
- Others mentioning users in content or complaints
- Professional organizations, businesses, government authorities, public sources

## How We Use Your Information

TikTok uses collected information to:

- Operate and administer Services
- Review, support, improve, and develop Services
- Train and test technology, machine learning models, and algorithms
- Personalize and customize features and content
- Provide shopping features and facilitate purchases
- Promote products and services through marketing campaigns
- Provide and improve advertising services
- Support social functions and user connections
- Infer additional user information
- Maintain safety, security, and stability
- Verify identity or age
- Communicate service changes
- Enforce Terms of Service and Community Guidelines
- Comply with legal obligations
- Aggregate or de-identify information

**Profiling**: TikTok states it does not engage in profiling resulting in legal or similarly significant effects.

## How We Share Your Information

### Service Providers and Business Partners

TikTok shares information with:

- **TT Commerce & Global Services LLC**: facilitating global, interoperable experience per Executive Order 14352
- **Payment processors**: PayPal, Stripe
- **Customer support providers**
- **Advertising, marketing, analytics partners**
- **Analytics service users**: creators, advertisers, sellers receive aggregated statistics
- **Sellers and their service providers**
- **Third-party platforms and partners**: for seamless sign-up, login, content sharing

### Within Corporate Group

Information may be shared with affiliated companies under common control with TikTok USDS Joint Venture.

### Business Transfers

Information may be shared during corporate transactions, mergers, acquisitions, reorganizations, asset sales, or bankruptcy.

### For Legal Reasons

TikTok may disclose information to comply with law, respond to subpoenas, court orders, legal process, law enforcement requests, legal claims, government inquiries, enforce applicable terms, and protect rights, interests, safety, and security of TikTok, affiliates, users, or the public.

### Users and the Public

Based on privacy settings, account information, user content, and usage information may be visible to other users and the public. Public accounts with unrestricted post visibility allow viewing by anyone and may appear on search engines, content aggregators, and news sites.

### Independent Researchers

TikTok may share information with qualifying researchers facilitating independent research.

### With Your Consent

TikTok may share information pursuant to user consent or direction, such as sharing email addresses for seller marketing communications.

## Your Rights and Choices

Users may submit requests to know, access, correct, or delete collected information via the privacy webform or physical address. Appeals of decisions may be submitted through the same methods. Users can update account information, request data copies, or deactivate/delete accounts through in-app settings.

**Authorized Agents**: Users may designate authorized agents with power of attorney or valid written authority to submit rights requests. TikTok will verify requests by asking for submission from the associated email or requesting account verification information.

**Cookie Controls**: Users may control some collected information by refusing or disabling cookies through browser settings. "Do-not-track" signals are not currently acted upon. Disabling cookies may limit service functionality.

**Advertising Settings**: TikTok account holders can navigate to "Ads" settings to manage how activity elsewhere online is used for ad serving. Non-account holders can manage TikTok Ad Network ads via webform or ad notices. Device controls may limit information collection access.

**Marketing Email Opt-Out**: Users can unsubscribe from marketing emails using provided mechanisms.

## Data Security and Retention

TikTok uses reasonable measures to protect information from loss, theft, misuse, unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. However, no data storage system or Internet transmission is guaranteed 100% secure. Third-party collected information may lack equivalent protections.

**Retention**: Information is retained as long as necessary to provide Services and for purposes outlined in the policy. Information is also retained to comply with contractual and legal obligations and for legitimate business interests, such as improving Services, enhancing safety and stability, and exercising legal defense.

Retention periods vary by information type and purpose. Account registration information is kept while accounts remain active, subject to user updates. Violators of Terms of Service or Community Guidelines may be immediately removed but information retained for violation processing.

Information may be transmitted outside the United States per applicable laws, including Executive Order 14352.

## Children and Teens

**Child Privacy**: TikTok provides a separate experience for users under 13, collecting only limited information. See the Children's Privacy Policy for details.

If TikTok becomes aware a child's information was collected outside the separate experience, it will delete the information and terminate the account per applicable law.

Users believing someone under 13 is using the Services (excluding the separate child experience) should contact TikTok at the feedback link provided.

**Parent/Guardian Requests**: Parents or guardians may request account deletion or data downloads for their child at the privacy webform. Depending on state residence, guardians may exercise additional rights over teen accounts. Guardian's Guide provides resources for understanding Services and available tools.

## Other Rights

### Sharing for Direct Marketing (Shine the Light)

California residents may request information once per calendar year about personal information shared with other businesses for their direct marketing uses via the privacy webform.

### Content Removal for Users Under 18

**California residents** under 18 may request removal of posted user content by contacting the privacy webform. Requests must describe the content and provide information sufficient for location. Removal requests cannot be submitted via mail, telephone, or fax. Requests may not ensure complete removal if content was republished by others.

**Connecticut residents** under 18, and parents/guardians of Connecticut residents under 16, may request account unpublishing or deletion following provided instructions.
