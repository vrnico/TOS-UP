# LinkedIn Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: November 3, 2025**

## Introduction

LinkedIn's mission connects professionals globally to enhance productivity and success. The policy applies to all Members and Visitors using LinkedIn Services, including LinkedIn.com, branded apps, and off-site services like ad platforms and plugins.

### Data Controllers
- **Designated Countries (EU/EEA/Switzerland):** LinkedIn Ireland Unlimited Company
- **Outside Designated Countries:** LinkedIn Corporation

## 1. Data We Collect

### 1.1 Data You Provide
**Registration:** Name, email, mobile number, location, password, and payment information for premium services.

**Profile:** Professional information including education, experience, skills, photo, verifications. Users choose what to share publicly.

**Posting/Uploading:** Forms, surveys, resumes, job applications, and calendar syncing data.

### 1.2 Data From Others
**Content/News:** Others may post about you; public professional information collected.

**Contact Information:** Calendar imports, business cards, contact associations, email headers (with opt-in).

**Partners:** Job titles, work emails from employers, recruiters, applicant tracking systems.

**Related Companies:** Data from Microsoft and affiliate services when users engage with them.

### 1.3 Service Use
Usage logging tracks visits, clicks, searches, app installations, and job applications via cookies, logins, device info, and IP addresses.

### 1.4 Cookies and Similar Technologies
Cookies, pixels, and ad tags collect device IDs across services. Non-Designated Country users: device tracking for ad targeting without prior engagement.

### 1.5 Device and Location
IP addresses, proxy servers, operating systems, browsers, device identifiers, mobile carrier info. GPS/location requires opt-in.

### 1.6 Communications
Message metadata collected. Automated systems manage responses and flag policy violations.

### 1.7 Workplace/School Information
Employers/schools purchasing premium services provide employee/student data and eligibility information.

### 1.8 Third-Party Sites
Data collected when users log in with LinkedIn or visit sites with LinkedIn plugins, ads, or cookies.

### 1.9 Other
New features may require new data collection; material changes trigger notification.

## 2. How We Use Your Data

### 2.1 Services
**Stay Connected:** Profile recommendations, connection suggestions, invitation sending, proximity-based networking.

**Stay Informed:** Content personalization, skill recommendations, network notifications about profile updates and achievements.

**Career:** Job recommendations, recruiter visibility, automated content recommendations.

**Productivity:** Collaboration tools, scheduling, message drafting, and meeting preparation.

### 2.2 Premium Services
Premium subscribers access customized search, messaging tools, and activity alerts for talent/sales/marketing. Limited profile exports permitted unless opted out. Enterprise services include TeamLink and LinkedIn Pages analytics.

### 2.3 Communications
Service notifications, security alerts, network updates, job suggestions, and promotional messages via email, SMS, push notifications, and inbox. Service messages cannot be opted out.

### 2.4 Advertising
Targeted ads both on and off platform using profile data, activity, inferences, and partner information. Members and Visitors can control personalized ad preferences but cannot opt out of all ads.

**Ad Choices:** Settings available for Members; separate controls for Visitors.

**Info to Ad Providers:** No personal data shared with third-party advertisers except hashed IDs, visible profile data, or with explicit permission. Ad providers receive signals when ads are viewed/clicked.

### 2.5 Marketing
Member data and content used for membership promotions and engagement communications.

### 2.6 Developing Services and Research
**Service Development:** Data used for research improving user experience and engagement.

**Research:** Social, economic, and workplace trend analysis. Third-party researchers may access controlled datasets; insights published as aggregated data.

**Surveys:** Optional participation; users can opt out of survey invitations.

### 2.7 Customer Support
Communications and data used investigating and resolving complaints and service issues.

### 2.8 Insights Without Identification
Analytics generate non-identifying statistics about members, professions, ad impressions, post engagement, and service usage.

### 2.9 Security and Investigations
Data used for security, fraud prevention, and investigations of legal violations.

## 3. How We Share Information

### 3.1 Our Services
**Profile:** Fully visible to Members and customers; visibility to others depends on settings and subscription level.

**Posts/Likes/Comments:** Public content viewable everywhere; group posts visible according to group privacy settings. Following actions visible; message read receipts subject to settings.

**Enterprise Accounts:** Employers see service usage but not job searches or personal messages.

### 3.2 Communication Archival
Regulated Members may archive communications outside LinkedIn for compliance purposes.

### 3.3 Others' Services
Account linking shares profile excerpts. Third-party services maintain separate privacy policies. Users can revoke links. Profile data may appear in third-party search tools and applications.

### 3.4 Related Services
Data shared across LinkedIn affiliates including Microsoft for service improvement, content development, and advertising services.

### 3.5 Service Providers
Third parties assist with maintenance, analysis, payments, fraud detection, and customer support under confidentiality obligations.

### 3.6 Legal Disclosures
Data disclosed when required by law, subpoena, legal process, or good faith necessity for investigations, enforcement, rights protection, or safety. Members notified when appropriate unless prohibited.

### 3.7 Change in Control/Sale
Data shared during sales, mergers, or control changes; continues under this policy unless user agrees otherwise.

## 4. Your Choices & Obligations

### 4.1 Data Retention
Data retained while account is open or as needed for services. Even inactive accounts retained unless closed. Some information depersonalized/aggregated.

### 4.2 Rights to Access and Control
Users can:
- **Delete Data:** Request erasure of all/some personal data
- **Change/Correct Data:** Edit profile or request corrections
- **Object/Limit Use:** Request stopping use or limiting processing
- **Access/Export Data:** Request copies in machine-readable format

Designated Countries residents have additional rights.

### 4.3 Account Closure
Profile visibility stops within 24 hours; data deleted within 30 days except where retention required for legal compliance, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or unsubscribe fulfillment. De-personalized information retained. Shared content remains visible; others' copies not controlled.

## 5. Other Important Information

### 5.1 Security
HTTPS and regular vulnerability monitoring implemented. No guarantee against breaches.

### 5.2 Cross-Border Data Transfers
Data processed globally using lawful transfer mechanisms.

### 5.3 Lawful Bases for Processing
Processing bases: consent, contract performance, or legitimate interests. Users can withdraw consent or object to legitimate interest processing via settings.

### 5.4 Direct Marketing
No third-party personal data sharing for direct marketing without permission. "Do not track" signals acknowledged.

### 5.5 Contact Information
Questions/complaints: contact LinkedIn online or by mail. Data Protection Officer contact available for Designated Countries. Additional remedies available regionally.
