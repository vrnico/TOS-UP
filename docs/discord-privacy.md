# Discord Privacy Policy

## Welcome

This Privacy Policy explains how Discord collects, uses, stores, protects, and shares personal information. Discord Netherlands BV serves as the data controller for EEA residents, while Discord Inc. handles everyone else.

Key principles:
- Discord prioritizes privacy and doesn't sell personal information
- The business model relies on subscriptions, paid products, and sponsored content
- Only necessary information is required; the rest is optional
- Safety is a core commitment through dedicated Trust and Safety teams
- Users have control over their privacy settings

## A Little Bit About Discord

Discord enables meaningful connections through voice, video, and text features for gaming communities and friend groups. Services are accessible via mobile, web, desktop, console, or any internet-connected device.

Users can share content in direct messages or community spaces of varying sizes. Content in larger, public spaces may be accessible to many people. Discord uses content from larger spaces to develop and improve services, including safety features. Users can control how their content is used through privacy settings.

The platform doesn't monetize through data sales — revenue comes from subscriptions and sponsored content like Quests.

## The Information We Collect

### Information You Provide

**Account information:** Username, password, contact methods (email/phone), birthday, and optional name or display name.

**Content you create:** Messages, posts, voice messages, custom emojis, files, and profile information. Discord generally doesn't store video/voice call contents, though this could change with advance notice. Screen-sharing thumbnails are retained briefly.

**Payment information:** Valid payment methods and billing details for purchases. Payment processors like Stripe and PayPal handle transactions. Last four digits of credit cards may be stored. Sellers may need to provide bank information and tax identification.

**Information from your actions:** Friend lists, server participation, roles, content moderation decisions, purchases, and engagement with bots and apps.

**Optional feature information:** Contact syncing, integrations, and third-party service authorizations (music streaming, gaming platforms) that display status information.

**Directly provided information:** Survey responses, support submissions, and feedback.

### Information Collected Automatically

**Device information:** IP address, operating system, browser type, and device settings like microphone/camera status.

**Usage information:** Pages visited, servers accessed, channels visited, activities engaged in, and features used.

**Additional automatic collection:** Information from ad interactions, referral links, and third-party platforms advertising Discord.

**Cookies and similar technologies:** Detailed in the Cookie Policy.

### Information From Other Sources

Discord may receive information from other users, third parties, social media interactions, advertisers, and data providers including demographic information and engagement metrics from external services.

## How We Use Your Information

Discord relies on different legal bases under data protection laws like GDPR:

### To Fulfill the Contract

**Providing services:** Processing images/audio for video calls, storing and displaying messages, creating accounts, facilitating purchases, and enabling linked features.

**Community commitments:** Monitoring for policy violations using reports, transcriptions, fraud detection, and malware scanning. Creating automated systems to detect and categorize prohibited content using reported content, violations, and widely available public information like usernames and server names.

**Contact:** Verifying accounts, two-factor authentication, and informing about product changes and purchases.

**Customer service:** Responding to questions and investigating issues.

### Legitimate Business Interests

**Service protection:** Maintaining security, preventing misuse, and enforcing policies.

**Company performance:** Tracking business metrics, financial reporting, regulatory compliance, and billing issues.

**Personalization:** Customizing experience with relevant communities, activities, and content. Users can control personalization extent.

**Sponsored content:** Surfacing Quests and sponsored formats. Users can limit this.

**Service improvement:** Understanding user interaction, desired features, safety development, and service enhancement through usage data and widely available content analysis.

**Advertising:** Promoting paid services directly and on other platforms; measuring ad effectiveness; improving future advertising.

**Contact:** Marketing communications about new products, feedback requests, and experiments. Users may opt-out; consent obtained where legally required.

### Legal Compliance

Discord retains information for potential legal claims, regulatory compliance, and auditing when required by law or court orders.

### With Consent

Discord collects and uses information with consent for marketing and personalized sponsored content where legally required. Consent can be revoked, though some services require it.

### Vital Interests

Personal data may be shared if someone's life is in danger.

## How We Disclose Your Information

**User direction:** Content sharing is controlled by user choices and server permissions. Third-party service integrations and bot usage involve information sharing. Goods/service purchases may disclose information to sellers.

**Vendors:** Payment processors, cloud providers (like Google), advertising platforms, and analytics partners receive limited necessary information.

**Legal requirements:** Information disclosed in response to legal requests; prior notice provided when feasible.

**Serious harm prevention:** Information disclosed to prevent death or serious harm.

**Policy enforcement:** Information disclosed to enforce Terms of Service, Community Guidelines, and protect rights, property, and safety.

**Related companies:** Information shared with parents, affiliates, subsidiaries, and commonly controlled entities.

**Business transitions:** Information disclosed during mergers, acquisitions, reorganizations, bankruptcies, or asset sales/transfers.

**Aggregated/de-identified information:** Anonymized data shared describing business, features, or promotions.

## Data Retention

Personal information is retained until no longer needed for processing purposes or legal compliance. Specific retention periods are detailed in Discord's data retention policy.

## How We Protect Your Information

- All information is encrypted in transit and at rest using TLS
- Voice and video communications are end-to-end encrypted (verification available in-service)
- Technical and administrative access controls limit employee/contractor access to nonpublic information
- Two-factor authentication is available for account security

## How to Control Your Privacy

### Be Aware of Discord Spaces

Users choose which spaces to participate in and what information to share. Awareness of server accessibility (Server Discovery, public invite links) is important since anyone can access certain servers.

### Customize Settings

User Settings (gear icon) allow privacy customization:
- Display current activity (games, music) in status
- Filter explicit content
- Control friend request permissions
- Limit data collection for service improvement
- Control personalization and sponsored content delivery

Privacy setting availability and defaults vary by age and location.

### Disable or Delete Account

**Disable:** Available via User Settings; stops new data processing but allows reactivation.

**Delete:** Permanently removes identifying information and anonymizes other data per retention policy.

### Manage Content and Servers

- Edit or delete messages and posted content (if access remains)
- Edit or delete servers with appropriate permissions
- Edit or delete channels with appropriate permissions

Content may be retained per policy and retention guidelines; legal obligations may require retention despite deletion.

### Access Your Information

Users can request a data copy in the Data & Privacy section of User Settings. Data arrives within 30 days in common formats (JSON, original upload formats).

## International Data Transfers

Discord is US-based, processing and storing information on US servers, with possible storage in other countries based on user locations and service providers.

**EEA transfers:** Discord uses standard contract clauses (Module 1 and Module 2) and relies on European Commission adequacy decisions or other legally compliant mechanisms.

**Data Privacy Framework:** Discord complies with EU-US, Swiss-US, and UK Extension Data Privacy Frameworks.

## Services Offered by Third Parties

Third-party developers create features like bots for content moderation and games. Third-party services may integrate Discord friends lists, messaging, or offer Activities. These must follow applicable policies including Developer Terms of Service and Developer Policy. Developers must have privacy policies disclosing information use.
