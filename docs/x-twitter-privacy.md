# X Privacy Policy

**Effective: January 15, 2026**

## 1. Information We Collect
- Personal accounts require display name, username, password, email/phone, date of birth
- Professional accounts need category and contact information
- Payment information collected for paid services
- Usage data tracked including posts, interactions, and device information
- Third-party data received from ad partners and integrated services

## 2. How We Use Information
- Operating and personalizing services
- Fostering safety and security
- Measuring service effectiveness
- Communicating about services
- Conducting research

## 3. Sharing Information
- Public posts visible to general audience
- Information shared with other users based on settings
- Third-party service providers assist with operations
- Advertisers receive engagement data
- Legal compliance sharing when required

## 4. How Long We Keep Information
- Profile information retained during account duration
- Payment records kept per applicable law
- Cookies maintained for up to 13 months
- Ad interaction data kept 12 months
- Suspended account identifiers kept indefinitely

## 5. User Controls
- Access and modify information via account settings
- Download personal data archive
- Deactivate accounts with 30-day restoration window
- Manage privacy preferences and settings

## 6. Rights and Legal Bases
- Data transfers use standard contractual clauses
- EU-US Data Privacy Framework participant
- Processing based on lawful legal foundations
- Users may contact supervisory authorities

## 7. X's Audience
Services not directed to children under 13. Users must be old enough to consent to data processing in their jurisdiction.

## 8-10. Policy Changes, General Provisions, Contact Information
For EU/UK residents, controller is X Internet Unlimited Company (Dublin). For US/other countries, controller is X Corp. (Texas).
