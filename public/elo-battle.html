How Type Advantages Work

Each company has strengths across 5 dimensions: Privacy (data collection, retention, sharing), Creator Rights (content license, AI training, monetization), Transparency (readability, honesty, terms changes), Safety (harassment protections, queer-friendliness, content moderation), and Accountability (dispute resolution, termination fairness, liability).

Each matchup randomly selects a battlefield dimension. The company stronger in that dimension gets a type advantage — like Pokemon. X/Twitter (Privacy: 3) can beat Instagram (Privacy: 1) on a Privacy battlefield even though Instagram has a higher overall score. This means no company is universally best — it depends on what you're measuring.

Each company's color is an RGB blend of their type profile. Blue-ish = privacy-focused. Green-ish = creator-friendly. Purple = safety-strong. The color tells you at a glance what a company's TOS prioritizes.
